# FinVantage MavPitch Phase I video - Production Script v2

Working title: "One Tower"
Recurring metaphor: your money is ONE tower of Jenga blocks. Each goal is a block (Trip = teal, Laptop = slate grey, Emergency fund = coral, Rent = mustard, the same four colours everywhere). A purchase is pulling a block. Other apps photograph the rubble after it falls; the Copilot speaks up one second before you pull. In v2 every business point lands on a tower beat: the tower collapses (problem), 5,000 tiny towers (simulator), tiles stack into one tower (dashboard), rival cameras look at the pile (competition), a field of towers (market), a coin column (revenue), the tower grows (go-to-market), four blocks reinforce the base (ask).

Rubric tags: R1 Overview/Problem, R2 Opportunity/Solution, R3 Product/Competitive Advantages, R4 Offering, R5 Marketing and Sales/Market, R6 Competitive Landscape, R7 Revenue Model, R8 Current Status/Go To Market, R9 Communication.

What changed from v1 (answering the consensus fixes and the fifth judge)
1. Revenue has its own 8 s beat (2:26 to 2:34): free to start, paid plan $79 to $99 a year, "our estimate" of about $72 left per $99, rival price anchors and the unverified $18 bank-data label on screen.
2. All-financials-in-one-place moved BEFORE the simulator and now has 14 s (1:30 to 1:44): one real screen recording showing net worth and the 12 accounts (checking, savings, CDs, retirement, loans, cards, mortgage), "no more five apps and a spreadsheet", the tie to the Copilot, and the life-event planner for 4 s.
3. AI Advisor has 17 s (1:49 to 2:06) in two spoken lines: trust and plain code that cannot invent a number, then two student questions, shows its working, never saves without a yes. The simulator line is cut from 15 to 8 words.
4. Competition (2:06 to 2:16) is shorter and fairer: Monarch as a short phrase, Rocket Money shown with its Rowan AI acknowledged (its card does not point only backward), then our different question.
5. Traction and team line added (2:34 to 2:46): "Built by four of us: 315 tests passing, 92 commits", plus "no users yet", plus the pilot. Founders and roles are in lower-thirds at 0:30 to 0:45 and each says a role aloud.
6. Market and go-to-market each have their own beat: market 2:16 to 2:26 (customer, 16M reachable, 16,000 to 48,000 users), go-to-market 2:34 to 2:46 (three acquisition steps).
7. Ask (2:46 to 2:55) says what $15,000 buys and the outcome, marked "proposal" on screen.
8. Compliance line said once (1:08 to 1:30): "Projections, not advice: it only suggests, and never moves your money." On-screen verdict wording is "likely range drops".
9. Explainer cut to 9 beats and made buildable in a weekend: flat 2D cutouts made in the editor, real Jenga footage reused from the founders shoot, and real screen recordings. Only 4 clips are AI-generated (3 in the trailer, 1 in the market beat), with a no-credit fallback for each.
Kept from v1: the tower collapse hook, the laptop vs Japan trip card, the rewind gag, the HackUTD proof, the honest status stamps.
