## How to record (laptop webcam or phone)

- **Framing:** landscape (horizontal), camera at eye level, head and shoulders in frame, a little space above your head. Look into the lens, not at the screen.
- **Light:** face a window or a lamp, never sit with a window behind you.
- **Background:** plain and tidy. A wall or a bookshelf is fine.
- **Sound:** a quiet room with soft things in it (a bedroom beats a kitchen). If you use a phone, keep it about an arm's length away; earbuds with a mic in a quiet room also work well.
- **Takes:** record three takes of your line in one file, with a short pause between them. I'll pick the best.
- **Settings:** 1080p or 4K, 30 or 60 fps. Whatever your device does is fine; I'll match them.
- Wear something solid-coloured (busy patterns flicker on video). Matching the team, for example all in dark tops, looks polished.

## What I do with rough clips

- Pick the best take of each line (Whisper checks the words), and trim to the first and last word.
- Clean the sound: remove background hum and hiss, cut "um"s and long pauses, and level the three voices to match.
- Colour-match the three cameras so the clips look like they belong together, and crop each to the Polaroid frame.
- Add the name cards, captions and music, then cut the intro onto the start of the explainer.

What I can't fix: a very echoey room, a dark or backlit face, or someone talking over the line.
Re-recording one take is quicker than fixing those.
